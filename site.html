Two ways to invest

FOCO — Franchise Owned, Company Operated

You invest in the infrastructure. ProPlayX runs day-to-day operations, marketing and staffing — effortless passive income.

FOFO — Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated

You own and run the facility, backed by our vendor network, booking software, staff training and ongoing guidance.
